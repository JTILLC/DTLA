What this app is for. Each shift, you record the state of every weigher head on your lines — which are running, which are offline, what the problem is, and whether it was fixed. That record builds a shared history JTI uses to service your equipment. You never lose work: everything saves to the cloud automatically.

01

Signing in

Open the link JTI gave you and sign in with the email and password JTI set up for your plant. There's no self sign-up — accounts are created by JTI.

  • Enter your Email and Password, then tap Sign In.
  • Forgot it? Type your email, then tap Forgot password? — a reset link is emailed to you (check spam).
  • If you see "Account not set up yet," you're signed in but haven't been linked to a plant. Contact JTI to finish setup.

Once you're in, the app opens straight to your plant — you only ever see your own data.

02

Overview — what needs attention

The app opens on Overview: what needs doing at your plant, before you go looking for it. Each row links to the screen that deals with it.

  • Heads offline — how many, on which lines, and which heads: “Line 2 · heads 4, 7 and 11”.
  • Heads running with known issues — working, but with something logged against them.
  • Pre-start walk outstanding — lines not yet walked for this shift.
  • PM due or overdue — by line.
  • Lines with no span record — never adjusted, or never logged.

Underneath, Heads at a glance shows every line as a block of squares, seven across, one per head — green running, red offline, amber offline-but-fixed, blue running with issues. It is the whole plant in one screen.

If nothing needs attention it says so, plainly.

Finding your way around

The tabs are grouped by what they are about:

This log
Overview, Current Log and Pre-Start — today's work.
This customer
Span Adjust, Parts/Boards, PM Log, Crew, Logins, Activity — records that outlive any single log.
Reference
Issue History and Factory Layout.

On a phone the row scrolls sideways — swipe it rather than hunting for a tab that has wrapped.

03

The screen at a glance

Across the top is a toolbar. The pieces you'll use most:

  • Customer: — your plant name and head count, shown as a locked label.
  • Daily Log — opens the list of logs. This is where you start a new one or open an old one.
  • Help — a built-in quick guide, always one tap away.
  • Export — save a PDF or data file of the current log.
  • A save status chip on the right tells you your work is safe:
✓ Saved 2:14 PM Saving… ⚠ Offline — will retry

Beside the save chip, a badge names who this tablet is logging as — Logging as J. Alvarez, or Nobody signed in — with Sign off next to it. Check it before you start work.

Below the toolbar, the section tabs are grouped into This log, This customer and Reference — see Finding your way around. On a phone the row scrolls sideways.

04

What the head colors mean

Every head is a numbered button. Its color tells you its state at a glance — this is the single most useful thing to memorize:

7
Green — ActiveRunning normally, no issues.
7
Red — OfflineDown with an unfixed problem.
7
Orange — FixedHad issues, all now marked fixed.
7
Blue — Active w/ IssuesRunning, but a problem is still open.

The same four colors appear in the head tables, the Dashboard, and the line list, so the whole app reads consistently.

05

Start a daily log

A daily log is one shift's record. A single day can have a separate log for each shift.

  1. Tap Daily Log in the toolbar, then + New Log.

  2. Pick the Date and Shift — 1st Shift 2nd Shift 3rd Shift.

  3. Leave Continue from last shift checked to carry over heads that are still offline and their open issues. (Photos aren't carried over.)

  4. Tap Create Log. The log is named automatically, like 2026-07-24 — 1st Shift.

To reopen an old log, tap Daily Log and pick it from the list. Each row shows its shift badge, date, and — if JTI attached a service report — an SR 2026014 tag.

06

The pre-start walk

The Pre-Start tab opens on a board — one row per line — answering the question an operator arriving for a shift is actually asking: has this line been walked today? Lines still outstanding are listed on Overview too.

  1. Tap a line that has not been walked yet.

  2. Work down the items, marking each OK, Issue or N/A. Some items carry a reference photo from JTI showing exactly what to look at.

  3. Add a photo where it helps, then submit. That line's row shows as done for the day.

It is not the PM checklist

Similar kind of items, but its own screen and its own log. Nothing here writes to the PM log, and a pre-start check never makes a PM look done.

Sanitation resets it

Once the machines have been handed over, every line needs walking again — a check signed before the wash-down was made against a machine that no longer exists in that state. See Ending a shift.

07

Mark a head down & explain it

This is the core task. On the Current Log tab, find the line and its grid of numbered heads.

  1. Tap the head's number. It flips to 7 red (offline) and a panel opens: Head 7 — Line 1.

  2. Confirm the Status — Offline or Active.

  3. Tap + Add Issue and choose what's wrong from the list: Chute, Operator, Load Cell, Detached Head, Stepper Motor Error, Hopper Issues, Installed Wrong, Radial Feeder, Booster Hopper Issues, or Other.

  4. Set the issue's state with the toggle, and type a note if it helps. Add Head Notes for anything general.

  5. Tap Done. Your work is already saved.

You can add more than one issue to a single head — tap + Add Issue again.

08

Add photos

A photo often explains a problem better than words. Tap the Photo button on an issue (or on the head itself) to take a picture or pick one — you can add several at once.

  • Save the log first. Photos upload to the cloud, so a brand-new log needs to be saved before the Photo button turns on.
  • Tap any thumbnail to open it full screen; tap the small red ✕ on a thumbnail to delete it.
  • Photos show up in the Dashboard and can be included in the PDF you export.
09

Mark an issue fixed

When a problem is resolved, reopen the head and tap the issue's status toggle. It cycles through three states:

Not Fixed→ Fixed→ Active w/ Issues→ Not Fixed
  • Once every issue on a head is Fixed, the head turns orange.
  • Use Active w/ Issues when a head is running again but you're watching a lingering problem — it turns the head blue.
10

Lines & head counts

Switch lines with the chip strip above the heads — one chip per line, each with a coloured dot showing that line's state, so you can see where the trouble is while you walk the plant. It scrolls sideways on a phone. On a small screen the Lines button in the bar at the bottom opens the same list as a full-screen picker.

To add a line, tap Add Line, give it a Line Name (e.g. "Packing Line A") and a Number of Heads. To change how many heads a line has, open Show Details and edit Head Count; reducing it removes heads from the end and asks you to confirm. Renaming a line, changing its head count either way, and clearing its weights all ask for a supervisor PIN.

A log with no lines yet

On day one the log offers Build your lines — or, if JTI has serviced this plant before, Copy from a JTI visit, which brings the equipment layout JTI already has on record. Building or reordering the plant's lines is a Site Lead action; the Set up lines link above the chips is where it lives afterwards.

Tip · Carrying work forward

You rarely re-enter your line setup. Starting a new log with Continue from last shift brings your lines, head counts, statuses, and open issues along automatically.

11

Ending a shift — handing to sanitation

When the machines go to sanitation, tap End shift → sanitation on the Current Log tab. This is the real boundary in a plant's day, and recording it is what makes the next shift's pre-start walk compulsory again — there is no separate switch to forget to set.

  • Heads still logged as down are counted for you before you confirm. Leave them — carry them over when the next shift starts and they stay on the record.
  • Who ended the shift, and when, is recorded on the log.
  • Every line needs its pre-start walk again before it runs.

Ended it too early?

A banner offers Shift is continuing — undo, for a shift that ended early or sanitation that is held up while the line keeps running. It removes a check that is currently required, so it takes a supervisor or Site Lead PIN and the reopening stays on the record — even though the boundary it cancels does not.

12

Dashboard & history

Two views help you see the whole picture:

  • Dashboard (button on Current Log) — every offline and active-with-issues head, grouped by line, with photos. A live banner up top reads 🔴 {n} heads down or ✓ All heads running.
  • Issue History tab — shows a head's past problems across earlier logs, so recurring trouble on the same head is easy to spot. Expand Past Issues on any head to see it inline too.
13

Export & share

Tap Export in the toolbar to save the current log:

  • Dashboard PDF — a clean report of every down/issue head.
  • Dashboard PDF (with Photos) — the same report with your photos embedded.
  • All Data JSON — the raw data, for backup or handoff.
14

"JTI Visit" entries

Some logs are created by JTI technicians during a service visit. In the log list they carry a JTI Visit badge, and when you open one you'll see:

👁 JTI service visit — view only. This log was done by JTI. Contact JTI to make changes.

These are read-only for you

The form is dimmed and can't be edited — that's on purpose, so a service record stays exactly as JTI left it. Your own shift logs are always fully editable.

15

Actions that need a supervisor

Day-to-day logging is open to everyone. A few bigger, harder-to-undo actions ask a supervisor or Site Lead to confirm with their PIN first:

  • Adding or removing a line
  • Changing a line's head count either way, renaming a line, or clearing its weights
  • Deleting a log, or deleting one permanently
  • Discarding a recovery backup
  • Continuing a shift that was already handed to sanitation

Two more take the plant's own top role, Site Lead, because they describe the plant itself rather than one shift's work:

  • Building or reordering the plant's lines
  • Adding or suspending a login

You'll see Supervisor or Site Lead — pick your name, enter your PIN, confirm. The entry records who authorised it.

It asks each time — with one exception

A supervisor stepping in to approve somebody else's action is asked every time: confirming who you are is not the same as approving this, and on a tablet the whole shift shares, permission should not build up in the background.

A Site Lead who is already the active person is not asked again, because they were being made to key a PIN for every single line they added while setting the plant up. Their PIN session is the proof — it lasts five minutes of inactivity, and the header names them the whole time.

Nobody set up yet?

If no supervisor or Site Lead has a PIN, these actions are refused with a note saying so. Set one on the Crew tab — or ask JTI to, if nobody there can yet.

16

Undo & recover

  • Reset or removed a line by accident? A yellow Recover button appears in the toolbar. Open it and tap Restore — the line comes back with everything on it: heads, issues, notes. Kept for 30 days, and the list shows how many days each one has left.
  • Deleted a log? It goes to the Recycle Bin in the settings menu, where you can restore it. Items there are removed after 30 days.

Removing a line is safe to undo

A copy is saved before the line is removed. If that copy cannot be written, the line is not removed at all and you are told why — so a line is never lost quietly.

17

Working offline

Signal drops out on the plant floor — that's fine. If you go offline you'll see a banner: "Offline — Your changes are saved and sync when you're back online." Keep logging; the save chip shows ⚠ Offline — will retry and everything uploads automatically once you're back on Wi-Fi or data.

The one rule worth remembering

Your work saves itself, constantly. You never need a "save" button for normal logging — just watch for the green ✓ Saved chip.

18

Crew, roles & PINs

The log used to say which account made an entry, which on a shared tablet meant every entry looked the same. The Crew tab fixes that: people are named, and prove who they are with a short PIN.

The roles

Operator
Logs work on the lines they are assigned to.
Maintenance
Any line, always — maintenance goes where the fault is, so they are never restricted.
Supervisor
Leads crewing, and confirms the bigger actions: adding or removing lines, deleting logs. Can also let someone work a line they are not assigned to.
Site Lead
A tick-box, not a role. Runs the crew list itself — who exists, their roles, their PINs, who is a supervisor — and can add logins for the plant. Usually your supervisor.

Someone can hold more than one role. The night tech is often the supervisor too.

What each role can open

The roles are cumulative — each one opens everything below it. Nothing is hidden. A tab above your level asks for a PIN instead of opening, so an operator who needs Parts/Boards hands the tablet to maintenance, who key their own PIN and carry on. Hiding those tabs would teach people the app is missing features and send them looking for another way round.

Operator
Overview, Current Log, Pre-Start and Span Adjust — the shift's own work.
Maintenance & Supervisor
those, plus Parts/Boards, PM Log, Activity, Issue History and Crew — the maintenance record.
Site Lead
everything, plus Logins and Factory Layout — who can sign in, and the plant's own map of itself.

Unlocking a screen takes the tablet over

Whoever keys a PIN to open a screen becomes the name this tablet logs under from then on — they are taking it over, not approving one thing and walking away. Everything logged next is theirs.

It starts open

Until your plant has set PINs up — or if nobody on the crew holds the role a screen needs — nothing is gated at all. A lock nobody can open is not security, it is a plant shut out of its own maintenance record. Restriction begins once you have said who is who. JTI logins are not subject to any of it.

Assigning lines

Each operator has a May edit row of lines. Tick the ones they work. Filing against any other line then asks a supervisor to approve it, and records that they did.

Tick nothing and nothing changes

An operator with no lines ticked can work every line — which is how every plant starts. Only tick lines when you actually want someone confined to them.

On shift, by line

At the start of a shift set who is on each line and press Save crewing. It applies to every device on the floor. Each line then shows its crew on the log screen, and everything logged afterwards records the crew for that line.

Who the app thinks you are

After you enter your PIN the app remembers you on that device, so you are not asked again for every head you touch. The header names you the whole time — Logging as J. Alvarez — with Sign off beside it, and each screen also shows Head changes recorded as … with a Not you? link.

Check the name before you start

It clears itself after five minutes with nothing happening, so a tablet put down on a bench stops being anybody. The clock is reset by use, not by when the PIN was keyed, so it never interrupts you mid-job. But if you pick a tablet up while a name is still showing and it is not yours, tap Sign off first — otherwise your work is filed under them.

19

Span adjustments

Each line runs on its own roughly 30-day clock. The Span Adjust tab lists every line with overdue at the top.

  1. Pick a line. Target (span) weights carry over from last time; current weights always start blank, so an old reading can never be logged again by mistake.

  2. Either type the weights, or tap Scan screen and photograph the weigher panel. It finds each circled head number and reads the weight inside that hopper, so it does not matter which way round the ring is turned.

  3. Scanned values arrive with a blue border and the photo stays on screen — tap it to zoom in and check them. Anything it could not read is listed rather than guessed.

  4. Press Log span adjustment. Nothing is saved before that.

20

Parts & boards

The Parts/Boards tab records what was replaced. A new entry starts from the last one — same line, head and part — but never the serials.

  • Type a part number or a part name and pick from the suggestions.
  • Or tap Browse to page through your machine's own drawings and tap the part on the exploded view.
  • The eye button hides the markers when they cover the detail you are trying to read; the list button lets you pick from a list instead of hunting a balloon.

Suggestions come only from your machine's manual, so a number that is right for a different machine cannot be offered by mistake. You can still type anything — it is simply recorded as unverified rather than ✓ manual.

Picking the right part

Type a part number or name and the app searches your machine's own manual — not a general catalogue — so a number that is right for a different weigher cannot be offered here. Or tap Browse to open the drawings and tap the part on the exploded view.

  • Several parts, one job — a board and its gasket and screws belong on one entry. Add them all; picking more later adds to the list rather than replacing it.
  • How many — each part has a quantity. It starts at 1 and shows what the drawing carries, so “2 of 10” records that you changed two of the ten screws.
  • Item numbers — the balloon number from the drawing is shown beside each part, so you can check it against the manual in your hand.
  • Typed a part that is not in the manual? That is fine — it is logged as typed and marked unverified, so the record shows the difference.
21

PM checks

The PM Log tab is the preventative-maintenance checklist JTI sets up for your plant. Some items carry a reference photo showing exactly what to look at.

Fill it in, submit it, and a supervisor can then sign off the check with their own PIN. That signature is recorded on the entry with their name and the date.

Why the checklist wording never changes retrospectively

Each submission keeps the wording it was signed off with. If JTI later edits the checklist, checks you already submitted still read exactly as they did on the day.

22

Logins for your plant

The Logins tab lists the accounts that can open this app for your plant — separate from the crew list. Crew are the names on your entries; logins are the accounts that get you in.

  • Add a login — enter their email. You get a link to send them, and they choose their own password. A Site Lead confirms with their PIN.
  • Suspend one — for someone who has left, or a tablet that has gone missing. Restore it later if you need to.

The badge shows how many of your allowance are in use. Most plants need one; a second or third is for another building or a night supervisor with their own device.

One login, many people

You do not need an account each. The tablet signs in once, and people identify themselves with their PIN — that is what puts names on the work.

23

Line activity & alerts

The Activity tab is one list of everything that has happened on a line — heads taken off and put back, issues fixed, parts replaced, span adjustments, PM checks and crew changes — newest first, each with who did it. The chips along the top filter by kind.

Taking a head offline, putting it back, or marking an issue fixed asks who you are. It asks once per device and then remembers you — for five minutes of inactivity, as everywhere else (see Who the app thinks you are) — and the name shows on the offline-heads list and on the dashboard, so the next shift knows who to ask.

What "Alert me" can and cannot do

Alert me notifies this device when a head goes offline on any line. It works while the app is open, including in a background tab. It cannot reach a closed app or a locked phone — do not rely on it as your only warning.

Appendix

For JTI admins

When you sign in as JTI (admin), the app unlocks tools that are hidden from customer logins. Admins are never asked for a supervisor PIN.

Customer picker
A dropdown to switch between every plant, instead of a locked single-plant label.
Add Customer
Settings menu → create a new plant with name, address, city/state, and head count.
Crew
Set up the plant's first Site Lead and give them a PIN. A plant cannot appoint its own first one — after that they manage the rest themselves.
Share
Generate a no-login, read-only link so a customer can view a visit's equipment status. Revoke it any time; view counts are shown.
Service reports
Attach a service report PDF and set its SR # (YYYY###) on a log.
JTI Visits
Any log an admin creates without a shift becomes a read-only "JTI Visit" for the customer.
Import JSON & Delete Options
Bulk data import and customer-level deletion — admin only, in the settings menu.
Parts manuals
Parts/Boards → link each line to one machine in the Parts Viewer catalog. A folder there is one machine. Part suggestions then come only from that manual — the line's model and serial are shown so you can see which machine you are binding, but the link itself is what scopes it.
Board types & PM checklists
Defined per customer and consumed by the plant's app. Both can be copied from another customer — a copy loads into the editor to adapt, and never overwrites until you save. Reference photos are not copied, because they belong to the other customer's storage.
First plant admin
Crew → tick a supervisor as admin and set their PIN. Only JTI can do this the first time; after that the plant manages its own PINs.
Supervisor sign-off
PM entries can be signed off with a supervisor's PIN — the one record here that is an attestation rather than a name from a list.
