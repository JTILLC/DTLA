What this app is for. Each shift, you record the state of every weigher head on your lines — which are running, which are offline, what the problem is, and whether it was fixed. That record builds a shared history JTI uses to service your equipment. You never lose work: everything saves to the cloud automatically.

01

Signing in

Open the link JTI gave you and sign in with the email and password JTI set up for your plant. There's no self sign-up — accounts are created by JTI.

  • Enter your Email and Password, then tap Sign In.
  • Forgot it? Type your email, then tap Forgot password? — a reset link is emailed to you (check spam).
  • If you see "Account not set up yet," you're signed in but haven't been linked to a plant. Contact JTI to finish setup.

Once you're in, the app opens straight to your plant — you only ever see your own data.

02

The screen at a glance

Across the top is a toolbar. The pieces you'll use most:

  • Customer: — your plant name and head count, shown as a locked label.
  • Daily Log — opens the list of logs. This is where you start a new one or open an old one.
  • Help — a built-in quick guide, always one tap away.
  • Export — save a PDF or data file of the current log.
  • A save status chip on the right tells you your work is safe:
✓ Saved 2:14 PM Saving… ⚠ Offline — will retry

Below the toolbar are three tabs: Current Log (where you work), Issue History (past problems per head), and Factory Layout (a map of your lines).

03

What the head colors mean

Every head is a numbered button. Its color tells you its state at a glance — this is the single most useful thing to memorize:

7
Green — ActiveRunning normally, no issues.
7
Red — OfflineDown with an unfixed problem.
7
Orange — FixedHad issues, all now marked fixed.
7
Blue — Active w/ IssuesRunning, but a problem is still open.

The same four colors appear in the head tables, the Dashboard, and the line list, so the whole app reads consistently.

04

Start a daily log

A daily log is one shift's record. A single day can have a separate log for each shift.

  1. Tap Daily Log in the toolbar, then + New Log.

  2. Pick the Date and Shift — 1st Shift 2nd Shift 3rd Shift.

  3. Leave Continue from last shift checked to carry over heads that are still offline and their open issues. (Photos aren't carried over.)

  4. Tap Create Log. The log is named automatically, like 2026-07-24 — 1st Shift.

To reopen an old log, tap Daily Log and pick it from the list. Each row shows its shift badge, date, and — if JTI attached a service report — an SR 2026014 tag.

05

Mark a head down & explain it

This is the core task. On the Current Log tab, find the line and its grid of numbered heads.

  1. Tap the head's number. It flips to 7 red (offline) and a panel opens: Head 7 — Line 1.

  2. Confirm the Status — Offline or Active.

  3. Tap + Add Issue and choose what's wrong from the list: Chute, Operator, Load Cell, Detached Head, Stepper Motor Error, Hopper Issues, Installed Wrong, Radial Feeder, Booster Hopper Issues, or Other.

  4. Set the issue's state with the toggle, and type a note if it helps. Add Head Notes for anything general.

  5. Tap Done. Your work is already saved.

You can add more than one issue to a single head — tap + Add Issue again.

06

Add photos

A photo often explains a problem better than words. Tap the Photo button on an issue (or on the head itself) to take a picture or pick one — you can add several at once.

  • Save the log first. Photos upload to the cloud, so a brand-new log needs to be saved before the Photo button turns on.
  • Tap any thumbnail to open it full screen; tap the small red ✕ on a thumbnail to delete it.
  • Photos show up in the Dashboard and can be included in the PDF you export.
07

Mark an issue fixed

When a problem is resolved, reopen the head and tap the issue's status toggle. It cycles through three states:

Not Fixed→ Fixed→ Active w/ Issues→ Not Fixed
  • Once every issue on a head is Fixed, the head turns orange.
  • Use Active w/ Issues when a head is running again but you're watching a lingering problem — it turns the head blue.
08

Lines & head counts

Switch lines with the Select Line dropdown or the Lines button. To add one, tap Add Line, give it a Line Name (e.g. "Packing Line A") and a Number of Heads.

To change how many heads a line has, open Show Details and edit Head Count. Adding heads is instant; reducing the count removes heads from the end and asks you to confirm.

Tip · Carrying work forward

You rarely re-enter your line setup. Starting a new log with Continue from last shift brings your lines, head counts, statuses, and open issues along automatically.

09

Dashboard & history

Two views help you see the whole picture:

  • Dashboard (button on Current Log) — every offline and active-with-issues head, grouped by line, with photos. A live banner up top reads 🔴 {n} heads down or ✓ All heads running.
  • Issue History tab — shows a head's past problems across earlier logs, so recurring trouble on the same head is easy to spot. Expand Past Issues on any head to see it inline too.
10

Export & share

Tap Export in the toolbar to save the current log:

  • Dashboard PDF — a clean report of every down/issue head.
  • Dashboard PDF (with Photos) — the same report with your photos embedded.
  • All Data JSON — the raw data, for backup or handoff.
11

"JTI Visit" entries

Some logs are created by JTI technicians during a service visit. In the log list they carry a JTI Visit badge, and when you open one you'll see:

👁 JTI service visit — view only. This log was done by JTI. Contact JTI to make changes.

These are read-only for you

The form is dimmed and can't be edited — that's on purpose, so a service record stays exactly as JTI left it. Your own shift logs are always fully editable.

12

Locked actions & the supervisor password

Day-to-day logging is open to everyone. A few bigger, harder-to-undo actions ask for a supervisor password first:

  • Adding or removing a line
  • Reducing a line's head count
  • Deleting a log (or deleting one permanently)
  • Discarding a recovery backup

When prompted you'll see Password required — enter the supervisor password and tap Confirm.

No password set?

If your plant hasn't been given a supervisor password, these actions are blocked with a note to "Ask JTI to set a password that lets a supervisor make these changes." Just contact JTI — only they can set or change it.

13

Undo & recover

  • Reset a line by accident? A yellow Recover button appears in the toolbar. Open it and tap Restore — reset lines are kept for 7 days. (Reset itself isn't password-locked, because it's this easy to undo.)
  • Deleted a log? It goes to the Recycle Bin (in the settings menu), where you can Restore it. Items there auto-delete after 30 days.
14

Working offline

Signal drops out on the plant floor — that's fine. If you go offline you'll see a banner: "Offline — Your changes are saved and sync when you're back online." Keep logging; the save chip shows ⚠ Offline — will retry and everything uploads automatically once you're back on Wi-Fi or data.

The one rule worth remembering

Your work saves itself, constantly. You never need a "save" button for normal logging — just watch for the green ✓ Saved chip.

Appendix

For JTI admins

When you sign in as JTI (admin), the app unlocks tools that are hidden from customer logins. Admins are never asked for the supervisor password.

Customer picker
A dropdown to switch between every plant, instead of a locked single-plant label.
Add Customer
Settings menu → create a new plant with name, address, city/state, and head count.
Password
Toolbar lock button — set, change, or clear a plant's supervisor password. Shows Password ✓ when one is set. Leaving it blank means only JTI can perform the locked actions.
Share
Generate a no-login, read-only link so a customer can view a visit's equipment status. Revoke it any time; view counts are shown.
Service reports
Attach a service report PDF and set its SR # (YYYY###) on a log.
JTI Visits
Any log an admin creates without a shift becomes a read-only "JTI Visit" for the customer.
Import JSON & Delete Options
Bulk data import and customer-level deletion — admin only, in the settings menu.
